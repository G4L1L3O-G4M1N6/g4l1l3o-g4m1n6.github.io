Eight Seconds: Bull Ride — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. Overview

Eight Seconds: Bull Ride ("the Game") is developed by Martin Suarez (G4L1L3O G4M1N6). This policy explains what data the Game handles, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Data stored on your device and in your Apple account

  • Save data: game progress and settings, stored in a local file on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • iCloud sync (iOS): if you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your save is kept in your own iCloud account so progress carries over to your other devices and survives reinstalls. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot access it. You can turn this off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Analytics ID: if analytics is on (section 5a), a random ID created on this install, used only to count sessions and gameplay events. It is not your advertising ID or any other device identifier, and it is deleted when you turn Analytics off.
  • Game Center (iOS): if you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads): may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GameAnalytics (game analytics, processing on our behalf): receives the random analytics ID above, gameplay events (for example tutorial steps, rounds and scores, which optional ads you chose to watch, which in-app purchases you made and their price), your device model, operating-system version and game version, and your IP address, which it uses to determine your country. It does not receive your advertising ID, and the data is not used for advertising. GameAnalytics ApS processes this data under a data processing agreement and keeps raw data for up to 1 month and player statistics for up to 18 months; data may be processed in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. See GameAnalytics' Privacy Notice.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit and Google Play Billing (purchases): purchases are processed by Apple or Google. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or account.
  • We run no servers that receive your data, and we do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with Google AdMob as described in section 5 and with GameAnalytics as described in sections 3 and 5a.

5. Advertising, tracking and your choices

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards. If you allow it, ads may be personalized using your device's advertising identifier and ad interaction data; this may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some US state laws.

  • iOS: the Game asks for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any cross-app tracking. If you decline, you still get the full game with non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EU, UK and certain US states: the Game shows a consent / privacy-choices form provided by Google before personalized ads are used. You can review or change your choices at any time from the Game's Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase removes non-optional ads.

5a. Analytics and your choices

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the Game is played (for example where players get stuck) so we can improve it. This is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps.

  • Everyone: you can turn analytics off at any time in the Game's Settings → Analytics. Turning it off stops collection immediately and deletes the analytics ID and any unsent data from your device.
  • EU, UK and Switzerland: analytics only runs if you agree to it in the consent form provided by Google (Settings → Privacy choices) or turn it on yourself in Settings → Analytics.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and your rights

Game data stays on your device until you delete the Game or its data. We hold no server-side data about you ourselves. Analytics data held by GameAnalytics on our behalf is keyed only to a random ID that we cannot link to you; turning Analytics off deletes that ID from your device, and GameAnalytics deletes the data on its retention schedule (raw data after 1 month, player statistics after up to 18 months). For other requests about analytics data, contact us. For data handled by Google or Apple, use their privacy tools.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest revision.
