Wildcatter: Oil Tycoon — Support

Need help with Wildcatter: Oil Tycoon? Email support@galileogamestudio.com and tell us your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Game's Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases such as Remove Ads come back on every device that uses that account.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes. When you are signed in to iCloud on your devices, the Game keeps your save in sync through iCloud automatically; if two devices disagree, the copy with more progress is kept. Game Center (leaderboards and achievements) is optional and signs you in quietly; it never blocks play. Make sure every device uses the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Ads in the Game come from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, the Game's Settings shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your ad consent at any time. On iOS you can also allow or stop app tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the Game removes its data from your device. If your save syncs through iCloud, that copy lives in your own iCloud account, not with us; resetting progress in the Game's Settings (where offered) also resets it. Game Center scores and achievements are managed by Apple in Settings → Game Center. We keep no data about you on our own servers, so there is nothing else to delete; if you have questions, email us.
