About the studio

Galileo Gaming is Martin Suarez, making small mobile games that respect your time. Every game is original, from the rules to the art, and most are about the rodeo, the ranch and the old West.

They play offline, need no account and never make you wait on an energy timer. Ads keep them free, and one purchase in each game turns the between-screen ads off for good.
